# GenesisL1 Editorial Asset Usage

These assets are provided for accurate editorial reporting about GenesisL1 and the July 21, 2026 Whitepaper Version 1.0 publication.

## Preferred use

- Treat `genesisl1-official-logo.svg` as the source of truth for geometry and orientation.
- Use the pure-black mark on white whenever possible.
- Use the clinical-navy mark or lockup on light neutral backgrounds.
- Use the white mark or lockup only on a dark, high-contrast background.
- Preserve clear space around the mark equal to at least one-quarter of the mark's displayed width.
- Keep the mark at least 24 pixels wide on screen and 8 millimeters wide in print.
- Use the supplied outlined SVG for scalable publication and the supplied PNG for raster workflows.

## Do not

- Redraw, rotate, stretch, crop or rearrange the mark.
- Add gradients, shadows, glows, outlines or new colors.
- Place the mark over a visually busy background.
- Use the assets to imply endorsement, partnership, network ownership or an official relationship that has not been stated.
- Remove qualification text from technical diagrams when it materially changes the meaning of a claim.

## Brand colors

- Pure black: `#000000`
- Clinical navy: `#1A2E4A`
- Medical blue: `#315A85`
- Ink: `#20252B`
- Mist: `#EFF4F8`
- Rules: `#CBD2D9`
- White: `#FFFFFF`
