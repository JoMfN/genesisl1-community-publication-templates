## Suggested captions

**GenesisL1 workflow:** GenesisL1 connects exact scientific objects, content-addressed Model NFTs, deterministic EVM execution, replayable results and programmable rights within one public state.

**Operational evidence:** Whitepaper Version 1.0 documents a network live since November 2021, more than 733,919 represented molecular structures across deployed collections, a second-generation deployment with roughly 229,000 PDB records and about one million sequences, deterministic GL1F inference and CIPNFT encrypted capsules.

**L1 coin:** L1 coin is the native GenesisL1 asset for transaction gas, replicated scientific state, inference, proof-of-stake security, governance, application settlement and community-pool funding.

**CIPNFT:** CIPNFT v1 records encrypted scientific-IP capsules, recipient-bound disclosure and an evidence-rich transfer chronology for attributable counterparties; a hardened successor and independent audit remain roadmap requirements.

## Accuracy note

The approximately 50 GB figure is reported logical scientific payload, not a measured validator-disk footprint. Checksum-complete corpus validation remains pending. CIPNFT's optional ML-KEM-768 mode is experimental and does not make GenesisL1 account signatures post-quantum. The whitepaper separates live, benchmarked, proof-of-concept, in-progress, planned and not-yet-independently-audited claims.
