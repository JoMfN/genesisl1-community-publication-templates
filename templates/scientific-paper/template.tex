\begin{document}
\MakeGenesisTitle

\begin{GenesisAbstract}
State the research question, approach, principal result, and interpretation. Keep claims
proportional to the evidence. Add keywords below when useful.
\end{GenesisAbstract}

\textbf{Keywords:} keyword one; keyword two; keyword three.

\StatusLegend

\section{Introduction}
Define the scientific context, the unresolved problem, and why the work matters.

\CentralQuestion{What precise question, objective, or hypothesis guides this study?}

\section{Materials, data, and inputs}
Describe samples, datasets, instruments, software, model versions, and inclusion criteria.
Use exact identifiers where available.

\section{Methods}
Describe the design and analysis at the level needed for independent examination or
reproduction. Distinguish preregistered steps from exploratory work.

\begin{GenesisProvenance}
List repositories, content hashes, software commits, environment versions, dataset IDs,
contract addresses, transaction references, or block heights when they materially improve
reproducibility. None are mandatory when irrelevant.
\end{GenesisProvenance}

\section{Results}
Report observations and analyses without mixing them unnecessarily with interpretation.

\begin{GenesisEvidence}
Insert the directly observed, measured, calculated, or implemented result.
\end{GenesisEvidence}

\section{Discussion}
Interpret the results, compare them with prior work, and consider alternative explanations.

\begin{GenesisInterpretation}
State the preferred interpretation and explain why it is presently warranted.
\end{GenesisInterpretation}

\begin{GenesisLimitations}
Describe uncertainty, possible bias, unresolved assumptions, negative results, and limits
on generalisation.
\end{GenesisLimitations}

\section{Relevance to verifiable scientific infrastructure}
Optional. Explain whether exact data, model identity, deterministic execution, rights, or
public provenance contribute to the work.

\begin{GenesisTrustBoundary}
A ledger may establish ordering, authorisation, byte commitments, provenance, or
deterministic execution. It does not by itself establish data quality, scientific truth,
biomedical validity, clinical safety, or correctness of interpretation.
\end{GenesisTrustBoundary}

\section{Conclusion}
Answer the central question at the level supported by the evidence and identify the next
most informative test.

\section*{Author contributions}
Use a recognised contribution taxonomy or a clear prose statement.

\section*{Data, code, ethics, and competing interests}
Provide the relevant declarations. Sensitive or identifiable data should remain off-chain
or use an appropriate protected architecture.
