GenesisL1 Community publication system
Template catalogue Proposed · community review
Exact objects · explicit provenance · honest boundaries

Formats for scientific work that can be read, tested, reused and remembered.

A proposed family of community templates for papers, reviews, letters, protocols and manuals. The formats share one visual and metadata language while preserving disciplinary freedom and the author's voice.

A supportive container, not a Procrustean bed.Use only the sections that help the reader. GenesisL1 alignment comes from clarity, provenance, openness, version identity and claim discipline - not from forcing every contribution into a blockchain or journal format.
