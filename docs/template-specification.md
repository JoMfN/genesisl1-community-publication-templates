# Template specification

## Scientific Paper

Best for completed original research.

Recommended modules:

- abstract;
- introduction;
- research question, objective, or hypothesis;
- materials, data, and inputs;
- methods;
- results;
- discussion;
- limitations;
- reproducibility and availability;
- declarations;
- conclusion.

## Community Letter

Best for direct community communication.

Recommended modules:

- reason for writing;
- context;
- what has been heard or learned;
- present understanding;
- open questions;
- proposed direction;
- invitation;
- acknowledgements.

## Scientific Review

Best for literature and evidence synthesis.

Recommended modules:

- scope;
- review approach;
- terminology;
- evidence landscape;
- thematic synthesis;
- convergence;
- competing interpretations;
- gaps;
- limitations;
- research priorities.

## Scientific Letter

Best for a concise observation, hypothesis, critique, response, early result, or conceptual proposal.

Recommended modules:

- context;
- central question or observation;
- contribution;
- evidence;
- interpretation;
- limitations;
- proposed test or next step.

## Protocol / Method Description

Best for reproducible procedures.

Recommended modules:

- scope;
- safety, ethics, privacy, and rights;
- inputs and prerequisites;
- parameters;
- procedure;
- quality control;
- expected outputs;
- failure modes;
- validation;
- provenance;
- change log.

## Manual

Best for task-oriented operational guidance.

Recommended modules:

- audience and supported version;
- system overview;
- quick start;
- concepts;
- installation;
- common tasks;
- configuration;
- security;
- troubleshooting;
- maintenance;
- glossary;
- support;
- version history.
