# Template specification

## Scientific Paper

- Two columns by default.
- Official logo on the first page only.
- Structured or unstructured abstract.
- Introduction, Methods, Results, Discussion, Conclusion.
- Equations, code, chemical compounds, schemes, tables, and wide figures.
- Optional exact-artifact, content-hash, ledger, and IPFS metadata.
- Active field profiles: blockchain, computer science, chemistry, biochemistry,
  and biology.

## Community Letter

Single-column direct communication with context, present understanding, open
questions, proposed direction, invitation, and acknowledgements.

## Scientific Review

Single-column evidence synthesis with scope, review approach, terminology, evidence
landscape, convergence, competing interpretations, gaps, and limitations.

## Scientific Letter

Single-column concise observation, hypothesis, critique, response, early result, or
conceptual proposal.

## Protocol / Method

Single-column reproducible procedure with inputs, parameters, steps, quality control,
failure modes, validation, provenance, and change history.

## Manual

Single-column task-oriented documentation with quick start, concepts, installation,
common tasks, security, troubleshooting, maintenance, glossary, and support.
